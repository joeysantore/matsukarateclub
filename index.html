Matsu Karate Club

Home · About

We’re a deliberately small karate club in New York City practicing Shotokan-based karate as a living art – technically rigorous and realistically applied.

Most karate exists at either end of a spectrum: belt mills teaching truly bad karate, or traditional dojos that preserve form but lose all substance. We’re neither.

If you’re interested in training with us, write to train@matsukarate.club.
