Matsu Karate Club

Home · About

We’re a deliberately small karate club in New York City practicing Shotokan-based karate as a living, evolving art – technically rigorous and practically applied.

Most karate exists at either end of a spectrum: belt mills where ranks are bought rather than earned, or stuffy, traditional dojos that preserve form but lose all substance. We’re neither.

Karate’s founders always intended for it to be an evolving art. We’re just getting back to that.

Most karate is small, boxed in by ego – openness is the antidote. We train with knowledge from wherever we find it, discard what doesn’t work, and treat every assumption as something worth testing. No one is above correction or beyond question. An open mind is the only real prerequisite.

Our training demands curiosity. We ask why, challenge norms, and consider where karate is going, not just where it’s been. Every session is a chance to understand something we didn’t before, so questions are the whole point.

We treat karate as a craft, with real lineage, technique, and standards. We strive for technical excellence and are critical about what that means. We care about getting it right, not for performance’s sake, but because precision is what separates a martial art that’s honest from one that only seems like it is.

Our community is the point. We grow together, push each other, and share in something we care about. We all have different backgrounds, bodies, and skill levels – but what we share is a desire to be here.
